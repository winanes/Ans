Who we are

Kids, Adults, Fighters

Twin City Boxing Club is a boxing gym at 345 Bailey Rd in Crystal City, Missouri, open to kids and adults. Classes are led by TJ Casey, a professional boxer and certified USA Boxing coach, alongside coach Craig Dalton.

Whether you want to get in shape or step into the ring, the club trains boxers for fitness and for amateur competition, including the St. Louis Golden Gloves. Email twincityboxingclubllc@gmail.com or message the club on Facebook for class times and membership options.
