Who we are

Built for Lockport Fighters

Spartan Boxing Club opened its Lockport gym at 970 E. 9th St. in November 2023. It's co-owned by trainers Oscar Hernandez and Kenny Bragg, who have been coaching boxers in the Chicago and Joliet area since 2012.

The gym has a full boxing ring, heavy bags and weight equipment. Kids, teens and adults are all welcome — beginners can start in group classes or book one-on-one training, and there's no age cap.

Call (779) 875-5098 for current class times and membership options.
