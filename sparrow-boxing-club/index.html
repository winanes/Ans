Who we are

Why Train With Us

Sparrow Boxing Club is a family-owned gym at 569 Knox Blvd in Radcliff, Kentucky, run by owners Kelvin and Shavonta Sparrow. The Hardin County Chamber of Commerce held its ribbon cutting in January 2022, when the club hosted the Kentucky Silver Gloves tournament. It offers classes, lessons and more for children and adults.
