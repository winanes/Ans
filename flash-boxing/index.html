Who we are

More Than a Gym

Flash Boxing and Activity Center is the East St. Louis gym of the Arthur Johnson Foundation, a nonprofit Arthur “Flash” Johnson and his wife founded in 2001 to serve local youth through boxing and mentoring. The State Street gym was dedicated in 2018.

Johnson grew up in East St. Louis, competed for the U.S. at the 1988 Olympics in Seoul, and holds amateur and professional world titles. Today he coaches the next generation — teaching that boxing demands discipline, and that the same discipline carries over to school, work and the community.
