Who we are

Why Train With Us

Mo's Gym is at 623 Washington Ave in Iowa Falls, Iowa. Owner Mike Emerson opened it in March 2021 in the former Salon Sovereign storefront, according to the Times-Citizen, with 24/7 member access, machines, free weights and cardio equipment.
