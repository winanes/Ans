Who we are

Why Train With Us

Mosburg Boxing Academy is a family-owned gym at 1603 S Main St in Joplin, Missouri, led by owner and coach Ryan Mosburg. KOAM News (June 2024) reported the gym is open Monday through Saturday with morning and evening classes, teaching competition boxing, kickboxing and MMA as well as amateurs — from age six up.
